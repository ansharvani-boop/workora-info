Personal desktop assistant

Your workspace, with continuity

A place to think, create, and keep your work.

Workora is a personal Windows assistant for conversations, writing, coding, research, and image creation. Its desktop workspace keeps projects and conversations on the owner's computer.

A personal workspace

Saved conversations, project files, a local editor, and tools for working with code. AI features use services configured by the owner.

Optional Google Drive storage

Connect your own Google accounts to back up Workora's public-source knowledge records and experimental model-update archives, and verify that saved copies can be recovered.

Why Workora requests Google access

Workora uses Google's authorization screen to connect an account you choose. The current desktop client requests permission to read Drive files and to create and manage files opened or created with Workora. Its backup and recovery features operate in folders marked as belonging to that Workora installation.

Google Drive stores backup files; it does not run the AI. Workora's Drive backup does not include private conversations or project attachments. Google account credentials stay on the owner's Windows computer and are not published by this website.

This site provides app information. Workora is currently a personal preview, with no public account registration or online access to the owner's desktop workspace.

Before connecting an account

Read the privacy policy for the data Workora accesses, stores, and shares, and the terms for the current personal preview. For support, contact the owner using the support email shown on Workora's Google consent screen.
