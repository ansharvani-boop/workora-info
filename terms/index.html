Current availability

Workora is an experimental personal desktop assistant operated by its owner. This website describes that app. It does not offer public registration, a hosted AI service, a paid subscription, or access to the owner's computer. Future availability to other people is not part of the current preview.

Authorized use

Use Workora only with accounts, files, projects, and devices that you own or are authorized to use. Follow the rules of any connected service. Connecting a Google account is optional and subject to Google's authorization and service requirements.

AI output and local tools

AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Check important information and review generated code before relying on it. Editor, terminal, and file actions can change the local computer; the owner is responsible for the actions they choose to run. Experimental learning results do not guarantee an improvement to the active model.

Backups and sharing

Keep independent copies of important work. A successful backup verification is not a guarantee against every future loss or account restriction. Share conversation snapshots only when you intend their contents to be accessible to anyone with the link. Revoking a link cannot remove copies already saved by recipients.

Privacy and third-party services

The privacy policy explains Workora's current data handling and Google Drive connection. Google, GitHub, and any additional service selected by the owner have their own terms and privacy policies. Workora is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, GitHub, or OpenAI.

Support and changes

The personal preview may change or become unavailable. For support, use the owner's support email shown on Workora's Google consent screen. Review updated app information and privacy disclosures when the app's behavior changes.
